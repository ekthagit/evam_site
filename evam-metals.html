How we work · 05 steps

From inquiry to discharge.

Scrap trading lives or dies on documentation. Every order follows the same five-step paper trail, so refiners and smelters know exactly what arrives.

01

Inquiry & LME indexation

Specify variety, tonnage, destination port. We respond with pricing tied to LME settlement minus refining margin.

Day 0 – 1
02

Sourcing & aggregation

Material aggregated from vetted yards across origin countries. Pre-graded, sample-photographed, held in bonded yard.

Day 1 – 7
03

Pre-shipment inspection

SGS or Bureau Veritas third-party inspection: weight, grade, contamination, radiation. Loading photographed.

Day 7 – 10
04

Documentation & payment

B/L, Certificate of Origin, packing list, SGS/BV report, market-specific compliance. LC presentation or TT.

Day 10 – 12
05

Shipment & discharge

FCL movement via Jebel Ali to destination port. Full track-and-trace. Discharge support and post-arrival docs.

Day 12 – 45
