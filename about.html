Where we're going · 03

Vision, mission, philosophy.

Three short statements that guide how we choose categories, build partnerships, and structure deals — the operating constitution of the company.

Vision

A multi-category trading house.

To become a globally-recognised multi-category trading company synonymous with reliability, quality, and trust — built from Dubai, serving customers worldwide.

Mission

Bridges between producer and buyer.

To bridge supply and demand by sourcing carefully selected products, partnering with leading brands, and delivering shipments with integrity, transparency, and operational efficiency.

Philosophy

Trade is relationship, not transaction.

Every shipment is part of a longer arc — a partnership with a producer, with a customer, with a market. We optimise for the next decade, not the next deal.

How we work · 03

Operating values.

Three practices we apply on every shipment, regardless of category or counterparty.

01

Quote what we can deliver. Deliver what we quoted. Refuse deals where these don't align.

02

Quality verified

Third-party inspection on every container. Documentation that holds up under audit. No shortcuts.

03

Long horizon

Optimise for the next decade, not the next quarter. Repeat business is the only business that matters.

Common questions · 06

FAQ.

A few of the questions we hear most often from new partners, distributors, and buyers.

  • We trade five categories: household commodities (honey, ghee, jaggery, salt) under Himalayan Natives; pet food and treats under Dogsee Chew; educational technology under Vidyardi; quartz and minerals under Benita Quartz; and copper and aluminium scrap across all major ISRI grades.
  • We're a free-zone company registered in IFZA Dubai. We serve buyers across the Middle East, Africa, Asia, and Europe — currently active in over fifteen markets including KSA, Qatar, Egypt, Kenya, India, Singapore, Germany, and the UK.
  • MOQs vary by category. Consumer goods start at 25–100 cases per SKU. EdTech hardware starts at 10–100 units depending on product. Quartz, copper, and aluminium scrap are sold by container load, typically 15–20 MT minimum.
  • We accept letters of credit (LC at sight or deferred), wire transfer (TT), and document-against-payment terms for established partners. Pricing is FOB Dubai by default; CIF available on request.
  • Every shipment is third-party inspected by SGS or Bureau Veritas for weight, grade, and contamination as applicable. Documentation includes B/L, packing list, certificate of origin, inspection report, and market-specific compliance certificates.
  • Yes. For partners willing to commit to multi-container annual volume, we offer exclusive territory rights, private-label packaging, and joint marketing programmes. Talk to our trade desk for term-sheets and reference programmes.
