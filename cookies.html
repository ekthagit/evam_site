This Cookie Policy explains how Evam Global Trading FZCO uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on evam.ae. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described here.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners about how visitors use their site.

Similar technologies include web beacons (tiny graphic files), pixels, and local storage — these work alongside or in place of cookies for similar purposes. Throughout this policy, we refer to all of these collectively as "cookies".

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use three categories of cookies on evam.ae:

Strictly Necessary Always on Essential for the website to function. They enable core features like navigation, security, and page rendering. These cannot be disabled.
Analytics Optional Help us understand how visitors interact with our website — which pages are most visited, how long people stay, and where they come from. All data is aggregated and anonymous.
Functional Optional Remember your preferences (such as language or region) to provide a more personalised experience on return visits.

Note: These toggles are illustrative. To opt out of analytics cookies, please use your browser settings or the opt-out links described in Section 6.
